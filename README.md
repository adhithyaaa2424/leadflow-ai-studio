# LeadFlow AI Studio

Build the complete LeadFlow AI Studio website according to the attached PROJECT LEADFLOW AI STUDIO specification:

A premium, modern and fully responsive website for fictional AI automation agency LeadFlow AI Studio, featuring:
- Official Parrot Green (#A8FF24) and Jet Black (#080B08) visual identity, Manrope & Inter typography, dark forest surfaces, and subtle glow accents.
- Sticky navigation bar with geometric logo, navigation links (Home, Services, How It Works, About, Contact), mobile menu, and parrot green CTA.
- Hero section with "AI AUTOMATION & DIGITAL SOLUTIONS" badge, headline "Turn Every Inquiry Into Your Next Client." (Next Client in parrot green), subheadline, dual CTAs, and an interactive lead automation workflow visual on the right.
- Trust and Value Proposition section ("Smarter Systems. Better Business.") with the 3 feature cards (Smarter Workflows, Better Lead Management, Less Manual Work).
- Services section ("Solutions That Move Your Business Forward.") featuring AI Automation, Lead Management Systems, Workflow Integration, and Custom Digital Solutions.
- How It Works process section (Discovery & Audit, Architecture & Design, Build & Integration, Launch & Optimization).
- Interactive ROI / Automation Savings Calculator.
- Metrics & Social Proof / Case Studies (+340% Lead Response Rate, 25+ Hours Saved Weekly, etc.).
- About Us & Philosophy section.
- Interactive Lead Capture & Consultation Booking Form with validation, state management, and toast notifications.
- FAQ accordion and comprehensive footer.
